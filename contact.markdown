Whether you're interested in collaborating on secure communication systems, custom scripts, or crafting unique hardware devices, feel free to get in touch!

#### Email:

You can securely reach me at: [amiscreant@protonmail.com](_aMiscreant@protonmail.com_)

### Please Note:

- **No File Attachments**: To ensure a secure and streamlined communication process, I kindly ask that you do not send files or attachments. This helps protect both your privacy and mine.

- **Response Time**: I aim to respond as quickly as possible, but please allow some time for a reply, particularly during busy periods.

- **Collaboration & Custom Projects**: If you're working on custom encrypted software, building hacking tools, or exploring hardware hacking devices, I’d love to hear from you! I'm open to collaborating on PenTest tools, Tor-based projects, IoT security systems, and much more. Let's push the boundaries of digital security together!
